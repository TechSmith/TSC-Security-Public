Sometimes people find things we’d rather they didn’t. Fortunately, some of those people take the time to let us know when we’ve made a mistake or overlooked something important.

This is our small corner of the internet for recognizing people who have sent us particularly interesting, useful, or notable security findings.

Not every report ends up here. This isn’t a leaderboard, scorecard, or a list of every security report we’ve ever received. It’s just a tip of the hat to the people who help make our stuff harder to break — or help keep all of us safer.

### Find something?
If you think you’ve found a security issue in something we make, we’d like to hear about it.

Check our [Disclosure Information](https://www.techsmith.com/trust-center/report-security-vulnerabilities/) for scoping details and how to get in touch.

And to everyone who has taken the time to report something responsibly — whether you’re listed here or not:

**Thanks for helping us make us safer.**
